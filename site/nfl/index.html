How to read it

Excitement index
0–100. Four-fifths Game, one-fifth Stakes. 50 is an average game this season; 80+ is one of the best few each week.
Game
What happened on the field: how much the win probability moved from play to play, counting late and sudden swings more than early drift, plus how close the finish was. Shown as a percentile against every game this season.
Stakes
Known before kickoff: both teams' records, whether both are winning, division game, rivalry. Will switch to playoff odds later in the season.
Season rank
Where the game lands among all 120 scored so far in 2026.
